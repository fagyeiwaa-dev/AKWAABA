A little about Ghana

Many stories, one country

Ghana is a country in West Africa with a rich cultural heritage and varied landscapes. Get to know its regions, languages, and places of historical importance.

83 languages

Ethnologue reports 83 living languages used in Ghana: 73 indigenous and 10 non-indigenous. Counts depend on classification.

2 UNESCO World Heritage properties

Ghana's inscribed properties are Forts and Castles and Asante Traditional Buildings.

Traditions and creativity

Discover Ghana's Culture

Ghanaian culture is diverse and differs among communities. These are a few ways to begin exploring it.

Traditions

Learn about the customs, languages, crafts, and community life of Ghana's many cultural groups.

Festivals

Festivals such as Homowo, Aboakyer, and Hogbetsotso reflect the histories and traditions of their communities.

Music and dance

Discover Ghana's varied musical traditions, rhythms, and dances, including the well-known Kpanlogo tradition.

Clothing and craft

Explore textiles and designs such as Kente and Adinkra, each with its own cultural context and meaning.

A taste of local life

Taste of Ghana

Ghana's food is varied. Here are a few popular dishes to look out for.

Jollof rice

Rice cooked in a seasoned tomato-based sauce.

Waakye

Rice and beans cooked together and often served with accompaniments.

Fufu

A pounded staple commonly served with soup.

Banku and tilapia

Fermented corn-and-cassava dough served with fish and accompaniments.

Red red

A bean stew commonly served with fried ripe plantain.

Kelewele

Ripe plantain pieces seasoned with spices and fried.

Plan your route

Explore Ghana by region

From the capital to the forest hills, each region offers a different experience. Use these highlights to shape your journey.

Greater Accra

City life, beaches, and history

Start in Accra for museums, markets, forts, coastal views, and a lively urban atmosphere.

Ashanti

Culture, crafts, and heritage

Visit Kumasi for Kente, local markets, palace history, and deeply rooted traditions.

Western

Forests, beaches, and wildlife

Explore the coast and rainforest regions for nature, scenic roads, and protected biodiversity.

Volta

Waterfalls and mountain views

Travel east for waterfalls, lake landscapes, and a cooler, greener rural atmosphere.

Best time to visit

November to March tends to be a great period for dry weather and festive travel.

Money matters

The Ghanaian Cedi is the local currency. Cash remains useful in smaller towns and markets.

Travel rhythm

Take time to enjoy local food, markets, and community festivals for a more authentic trip.
