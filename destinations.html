The Manhyia Palace Museum shares Asante royal history through regalia, photographs, furniture, and other objects connected with the Asante kings.

Kumasi ZooKumasi

Location

Kumasi, Ashanti Region.

Short history

The Asanteman Council established Kumasi Zoo in 1957 as a place for wildlife conservation and public learning. It remains a green wildlife site in the centre of Kumasi.

Read more at Visit Ghana

Lake BosomtweAshanti Region

Location

Ashanti Region, south-east of Kumasi.

Short history

Lake Bosomtwe formed in a meteorite-impact crater and is Ghana?s only natural inland lake. It is also recognized as a biosphere reserve, and communities around it maintain their own cultural traditions.

Location

On the Black Volta in the Bono Region.

Short history

The Bui hydroelectric project was built on the Black Volta, on the boundary between the Bono and Savannah regions. Its generating station was commissioned in 2013.

Location

Near Kintampo in the Bono East Region.

Short history

Kintampo Waterfalls, also known in older accounts as Sanders Falls, is formed by cascades on the Pumpum River, a tributary of the Black Volta. The falls have long been a natural landmark near Kintampo.

Central Region

Cape Coast CastleCape Coast

Location

Cape Coast, Central Region.

Short history

The Swedes built the first permanent fort here in 1653. The British captured it in 1665 and later expanded it into a castle; the site is closely tied to the history of the Atlantic slave trade.

Read more at Ghana Museums and Monuments Board

Location

Central Region, north of Cape Coast.

Short history

Kakum protects rainforest within the Assin Attandanso area. Its best-known visitor feature is a canopy walkway above the forest, while the park?s wider purpose includes protecting forest habitat and wildlife.

Read more at Visit Ghana

Elmina CastleElmina

Location

Elmina, Central Region.

Short history

Portuguese forces founded St. George?s Castle, now Elmina Castle, in 1482. It became a major European trading stronghold, and its history includes the trade in enslaved people.

Bunso, Eastern Region.

Bunso Arboretum combines native forest with plants introduced for research by the Plant Genetic Resources Centre. It is used for plant education and conservation as well as nature visits.

Aburi Botanical GardensAburi

Location

Aburi, Eastern Region.

Short history

After earlier proposals, the Aburi Botanical Gardens were established in 1890 during the colonial period. They became a place for plant collections and study on the Akuapem hills.

Near Boti in the Eastern Region.

Boti is a seasonal twin waterfall in the Eastern Region. Local stories describe the two falls as male and female; this is a community tradition rather than a documented historical fact.

Location

Akosombo, Eastern Region, on the Volta River.

Short history

The Akosombo Dam was constructed between 1961 and 1965 on the Volta River. Its reservoir created Lake Volta, and the dam became a major part of Ghana?s hydroelectric history.

Read more at Volta River Authority

Savannah Region

Larabanga MosqueLarabanga, Savannah Region

Location

Larabanga, West Gonja District, Savannah Region, near Damongo.

Short history

Larabanga Mosque is a historic mud-and-stick mosque built in a Sudano-Sahelian style and remains important to the local Muslim community. Its often-repeated 15th-century date and founder stories are uncertain, so they should be treated as tradition rather than proven fact.

Read more at Visit Ghana

Photo gallery

Mole National ParkSavannah Region

Location

Savannah Region, in northern Ghana.

Short history

Mole was first set aside as a protected area in 1958 and became a national park in 1971. It protects savannah and riverine habitats and is especially known for its wildlife, including elephants.

Read more at Visit Ghana
